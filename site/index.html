Customer Support Agent

Support that resolves, not just replies.

SupportAgent resolves conversations through grounded retrieval, controlled account actions, and reliable escalation.

GitHub↗

Principle

The Agent is only as useful as the boundaries around it are clear.

01

Ask before inventing

Clarification is safer than fabricated account facts.

02

Audit every side effect

Tool actions are idempotent, permissioned, and observable.

03

Escalation is a feature

A reliable handoff is better than a confident wrong answer.

Loop

From raw signals to measurable behavior, each layer contributes a distinct part of the Agent system.

01 / DATA

Customer context

Conversations, tickets, policies, account and order events.

02 / MODELS

Understand intent

Intent detection, response planning, policy reasoning.

03 / TRAINING

Learn resolution

Resolved-case SFT and policy-adherence preference learning.

04 / INFERENCE

Live assistance

Streaming responses, retrieval, action selection.

05 / HARNESS

Safe actions

Order, refund, CRM and escalation tools.

06 / EVAL

Resolution quality

Accuracy, safety, compliance and successful resolution.
